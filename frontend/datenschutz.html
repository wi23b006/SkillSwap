Privacy policy (draft)

This notice is a draft for testing.

What we store

  • Email and a hashed password for your account
  • Your profile: display name, bio, city, contact email
  • The skills you offer or want to learn

Who sees it

Other users see your profile and skills. Your contact email stays private until you and the other user have both liked each other.

Deleting your data

When your account is deleted, your profile and skills are deleted with it.
